## Algorithms
**Algorithm**
#card 
An algorithm is a sequence of precise instruction s which leads to a solution - also known as a recipe, method, directions, procedure or routine.
^q-r2d6

Instructions may well be in terms of a programming language or a human language. 

A computer program is simply an algorithm expressed in a language that a computer can understand.
